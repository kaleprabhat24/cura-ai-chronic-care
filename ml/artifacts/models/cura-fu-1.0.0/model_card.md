# Model card — cura-fu-1.0.0

> **Synthetic-data evaluation, not clinical validation.** All patients, events and outcomes are simulated. These numbers describe how well the models recover structure that the simulator put into the data. They are not evidence of real-world performance or clinical benefit.

## Model
- **Type:** Logistic regression (selected by a pre-declared validation rule; see the model report)
- **Prediction target:** follow-up still incomplete 30 days after the due date, predicted 14 days before the due date.
- **Output:** calibrated probability (platt calibration fitted on validation data),
  a risk tier (High ≥ 0.569, Medium ≥ 0.241),
  and per-prediction explanations (logistic_regression_contributions, log-odds contributions).
- **Features:** 44 columns, feature spec fs-1.0
  (see `feature_spec.json`). All features are computed from records recorded before the prediction time.

## Intended use
Ranking eligible chronic-care follow-ups for outreach in a **demonstration** application on synthetic data.
The model predicts follow-up non-completion. It does **not** predict disease deterioration, and it does
not diagnose or recommend treatment.

## Not intended for
Clinical decision-making, real patients, or any claim of real-world effectiveness.

## Training data
Synthetic cohort `v1`
(data fingerprint `b34327852b0270f6…`).
Trained on 11,086 episodes from [2020, 2021, 2022]; calibrated on
1,546 validation episodes. Tested on held-out patients in
2024.

## Test results (held-out patients, synthetic)
PR-AUC 0.647 [0.588, 0.702], ROC-AUC 0.791 [0.751, 0.821],
Brier 0.158 [0.143, 0.175], precision at 15% capacity
0.735 [0.641, 0.808], calibration slope
1.09, ECE 0.049. Test prevalence 0.291 (n = 777).

## Explanations
Contributions are on the log-odds scale of the uncalibrated model. They are not percentages of risk.
They show which recorded data the model relied on. They are associations, not causes.

## Limitations
- Synthetic data generated by a simplified simulator; real-world relationships will differ.
- The label reflects outcomes under the simulated historical outreach policy. It is not a causal
  estimate of what outreach achieves.
- Performance can drift. Re-evaluate on new data before reuse. Check the feature spec and
  library versions in `metadata.json`.
